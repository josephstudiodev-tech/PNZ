What is Project Net Zero?

Project Net Zero is a smart system created by engineering students at HITAM. It watches over our city to make sure factory chimneys and road vehicles don't release bad poison gases into the air we breathe!

🤖

Smart Sensors

Small gadgets placed on buildings that act like electronic noses. They sniff the air every 10 seconds to check for toxic gases.

📷

AI Camera Eyes

Special cameras powered by AI look at factory chimneys to catch dark illegal smoke plumes even during the middle of the night.

🚨

Instant Alerts

If the air gets dangerous, the system automatically sends an emergency email and message to government pollution officers!

Our 4-Year Plan for Clean Air

Here is how we plan to grow Project Net Zero over 4 years to make the whole city of Hyderabad cleaner and safer!

Year 1

Prototype Development

Building basic IoT gas sensors and testing gas detection accuracy at HITAM campus.

Impact Level:
🟢 Level 1: Initial Testing
Year 2

Pilot Implementation

Installing prototype nodes in industrial micro-zones like Jeedimetla and Patancheru.

Impact Level:
🔵 Level 2: Ground Deployment
Year 3

System Enhancement

Advanced AI camera integration for smoke detection & seamless sensor synergy.

Impact Level:
🟡 Level 3: AI & Camera Synergy
Year 4

Expansion & Sustainability

Expanding to all industrial sectors across Hyderabad with solar power backup.

Impact Level:
